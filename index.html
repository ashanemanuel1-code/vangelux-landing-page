Why VANGELUX exists

What if the clothes we wear could do more than make a fashion statement? What if they could carry a message, start conversations about Jesus, and remind us daily of whom we represent?

Speaking openly about faith can sometimes be difficult. VANGELUX creates another way to share it, through thoughtful, Gospel-centred design. Because the message isn’t only for the person looking at your shirt. It’s also for the person wearing it.

A message to the world

Share biblical truth through meaningful design, spark respectful conversations about Jesus, and offer hope to someone who needs it.

A reminder to yourself

Be mindful of your words and choices, treat people with love and grace, and stand firm in faith, even when no one is watching.

EVANGEL + LUX

VANGELUX joins the Gospel (evangel) and light (lux): carrying the light of Christ into everyday life.
